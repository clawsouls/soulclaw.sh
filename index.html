Why SoulClaw?

📵

AI That Stays on Your Phone

Run local models fully on-device. Works offline, and your conversations never leave your hand.

🎭

A Soul, Not a Chatbot

Personas defined in SOUL.md — an open format. Your agent's identity is a file you own and carry anywhere.

🧠

It Remembers You

Semantic memory, stored on your device. Preferences, plans, and past conversations carry over.

🛠

An Agent That Does Things

Web search, files, clipboard, notifications — real tools, with every call shown and under your approval.

🔑

Your Key, Your Models

Bring your own key for Claude, GPT, or Gemini — or use free models. No markup, no middleman.

🧩

Teach It New Skills

🛠 Beyond the Phone

The same souls and memory, wherever you work. SoulClaw ships as a CLI and a VSCode extension too.

⌨️ CLI

Terminal-first. Semantic memory, Soul Browse, automation.

npm install -g soulclaw

🧩 VSCode

AI engine in your IDE. Chat, SoulScan, Swarm Memory.

ext install clawsouls.soulclaw-vscode
